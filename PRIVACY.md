# Basic Art Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-08

Basic Art is an image editor that works entirely on your device. This policy is short because there is very little to say.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Basic Art does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data or usage data. There is nothing for us to collect because nothing ever leaves your device.

## No network connections

Basic Art never connects to the internet.

- On Android, the app does not even request the `INTERNET` permission, so the system will not let it go online.
- On iOS, the app makes no network calls.

Fonts, color palettes, text presets and everything else the app uses are bundled inside it. Nothing is downloaded while you use it.

## No ads, analytics or accounts

There are no ads, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash-reporting services, no accounts and no sign-in. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

## Your projects

- Projects are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not written to your shared storage and are not backed up to any server we run.
- When you add a photo to a project, the app copies that photo into the project. It does not read or change anything else in your photo library or files.
- Uninstalling Basic Art deletes all of its projects. You can also delete them yourself at any time, one by one from the home screen or all at once from **Settings → Clear all project history**.
- Images you export are saved where you choose (your Photos or Pictures folder, Files, or a share target). They are yours, and they stay even if you delete a project or uninstall the app.

Your projects can be included in your device's own system backups (Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud / computer backups on iOS), according to your device settings, so they can come back if you restore or move to a new device. Those backups are made by Google or Apple under your account, not by Basic Art. Basic Art itself never sends anything anywhere. (Android limits each app's backup size, so very large projects may not all be included; exporting is the surest way to keep a copy.)

## Permissions, and why

**Android**

- **Picking photos:** the app uses the system photo picker, which needs no permission. You choose exactly which images to share with the app.
- **Saving exports:** on Android 10 and newer, no permission is needed. On Android 9 and older, the app asks for storage write permission (`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`) only so it can save your exported image to your Pictures folder. It is never used to read your files.

**iOS**

- **Picking photos:** the app uses the system photo picker, which needs no permission.
- **Saving exports:** the app asks for *add-only* Photos access so it can save your exported image to your library. It cannot see or read the photos already there.

You can decline any of these and still use the app. You can always share or save exports through the system share sheet instead.

## Children

Basic Art collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted in this repository with a new effective date.
